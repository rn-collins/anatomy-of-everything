# The Anatomy of… a Hug

Production source brief · 23 September 2026 · Four distinct reusable images · Exported

## Decision

Luna Lu’s *Anatomia do abraço* (2016) inspired this episode, but neither the [artist’s installation photograph](https://www.instagram.com/p/BdC1PBbAddv/) nor the photograph in the [2016 exhibition catalog](https://www.aic-iac.org/wp-content/uploads/CATALOGO5o-SALAO-CERAMICA.pdf) carries a public reproduction license. The catalog credits Marcello Kawase for its photography, but that credit is not a license. **Neither image belongs in the uploadable carousel.** Link to the original in the post caption or source notes; do not present an image by another artist as Lu’s work.

This four-slide version is an original visual essay about hugs, using four individually licensed works. It does not purport to interpret Lu’s specific sculpture or assign intentions to her.

Place text in clear space rather than obscuring the subject. No synthetic imagery or reconstructed photograph is part of this package.

## Source verification

- [The Met’s object page](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/334656) labels Rodin’s exact drawing Public Domain and links a downloadable image. Do not substitute its separately cataloged 1979 cast, whose image has different restrictions.
- [Wellcome’s exact McConkey item](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/yyqj4nc3) and [exact M0012200 item](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/nw8n5cbr) each say CC BY 4.0 and commercial use permitted, with a full-size JPG link and prescribed credit.
- [NGA’s exact Master MZ object](https://www.nga.gov/artworks/3394-embrace) states that its downloadable media is free and in the public domain.
- A live-person CC0 hugging photo and an Unsplash photo were considered, but no model release was established for a branded promotional treatment; they are not in the selected set.
