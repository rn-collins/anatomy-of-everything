## Editorial system

Each episode starts with a visual question, moves through anatomy, and returns to the human action. Keep the original image title/creator and CC license in each platform’s description or linked article. A photograph of a historic model is introduced as a historic model; a photograph of training equipment is introduced as an example of equipment. Sainz’s own account supports claims about his training. The anatomy and kit photographs illustrate different parts of the story.

Build one illustrated article per episode on beehiiv/web as the durable destination for sources and the longer interpretation. Beehiiv can publish a post to web and email, with different layout previews for each. Give Pinterest and Threads a direct article URL once the article exists; retain complete attribution wherever the media travels. I followed four works of art from the shape of an embrace back to the people inside it. What detail makes a hug feel like *your* memory?

Attach Rodin’s *The Embrace* with its compact credit. Add the illustrated article URL when live, with the complete source/CC credits available in that destination.

Sainz has described neck weights, a weighted karting helmet and trainer-applied resistance. I traced that story through racing photos, a historical wax model and a neck-training kit. What do you notice next time the car turns?
