## Removed candidates and terms

The Oscar Carrascosa / Red Bull Content Pool pictures of Sainz’s actual 2017 training are excluded because [Red Bull’s public terms](https://policies.redbull.com/r/redbullcontentpool/terms/en) reserve promotional or advertorial use for separate licensing; credit is no substitute. The previous Pexels athlete photo was also removed because its original could not be downloaded from the available environment. A prior brief described a Jen Ross racing photograph as a face portrait and an el_ave race photograph as a high-angle helmet view; inspection of those exact files disproved both descriptions. Neither file appears in the final five-slide package.
